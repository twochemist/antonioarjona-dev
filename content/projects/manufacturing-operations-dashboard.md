## Purpose

This design study explores how production information could be organised around availability, performance and quality, the three factors used in Overall Equipment Effectiveness (OEE).

## Scope

The proposed interface would bring together production counts, planned production time, stop durations and quality losses. The aim is to help a user understand the inputs behind an indicator and identify which loss to investigate next.

## Design priorities

- Show the reporting period and the definitions behind each metric.
- Keep source data and calculated indicators distinguishable.
- Make missing or stale data visible.
- Allow the user to move from a summary to the events behind it.

## Current state

This is a concept and requirements study, illustrated by an overview graphic. It does not demonstrate a live connection to production equipment, a predictive-maintenance model or an AI analytics service.

A working prototype and a reproducible sample dataset would be needed to evaluate the interface and calculations. No production results are claimed.
